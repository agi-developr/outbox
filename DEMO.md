# Outbox — 60-second demo script

Link: https://agi-developr.github.io/outbox/

## The one-liner
"Every email client still makes you read. Outbox deletes the inbox."

## Beat 1 — the boot (0:00–0:10)
Refresh the page in front of them. Let the boot log run.
Say: "This is what happened overnight. It read 47 messages. Nothing was deleted, nothing was sent."

## Beat 2 — the lanes (0:10–0:25)
"There is no message list here. These four columns are not folders — they are the agent's decisions.
Handled is done. Reply ready has the email already written. Waiting on is being watched.
Needs you is the only place I have to look."

## Beat 3 — the rule (0:25–0:40)
Point at Marcus Bell, Sunset Inn, asking for $180 instead of $299.
"Notice there is no draft on this one. Anything that moves money or a commitment gets no draft on purpose.
The agent found the pattern — third pilot asking for the same cut — and then stopped."
Click the card. Show the reasoning drawer.
"Every card tells you why it landed where it did. A wrong lane is a bug you can see."

## Beat 4 — the interaction (0:40–0:50)
Type into the Needs-you quick field: `Hold the price, throw in after-hours routing`
Hit enter.
"I type the decision, not the email. It writes the sentences in my voice."

## Beat 5 — the live moment (0:50–1:00)
Click "Deliver a new email" in the top right.
Let the thinking bar run — reading, checking calendar and CRM, scoring, routing.
Card lands in Reply ready.
Click it again. Second one lands in Needs you, with no draft, because it asks for regional exclusivity.
"Same agent, two emails, two different answers. That is the whole product."

## If a judge asks "is this real?"
Be straight: the triage output is pre-computed, the UI is real and deployed, and the plumbing
that makes it live is AgentMail (agentmail.to) — instant API-key inboxes, no Gmail OAuth review.
That is the next 30 minutes of work, not the next 30 days.

## If a judge asks "how is this different from Superhuman / Sortd / a kanban Gmail plugin?"
Those speed up sorting a list you still have to read. This one has no list.
The unit on the board is a decision, not a message.
